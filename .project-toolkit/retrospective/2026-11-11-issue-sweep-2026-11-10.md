## Phase 1: Insights Generated

### Five Whys

**Failure 1: rate-limit exhaustion.**
1. Why did drivers stall? They waited on rate-limit reset timers (REPORTED).
2. Why was the budget gone? About 31 agents polled checks and threads on one token (REPORTED count; OBSERVED the GraphQL refusals).
3. Why were there 31? The plan fanned out one delegate per issue and per PR, and the brief sized the wave by issue count.
4. Why was there no cap? The cap of 4 concurrent delegations is in `templates/agents/orchestrator.shared.md:329`, and `COMMON_BRIEF.md` omits it.
5. Why does the omission recur? The cap is template text and a memory, not a check. Root cause: a soft requirement with no artifact a tool can inspect. This is the cross-cutting theme in `FAILURE-MODES.md`.

**Failure 2: long-lived children degrade.**
1. Why did the verdicts go bad? The children had run many turns (REPORTED: 58 runs) and lost context.
2. Why were they kept? Resuming one child by `thread_send` looked cheaper than a fresh brief.
3. Why was that wrong? The sweep log shows the cost: blank tool results, a lost PR URL, and a wrong summary.
4. Why did it surface late? Verdicts were read before GitHub state was checked.
5. Root cause: the child summary was treated as evidence. Class 9.

**Failure 3: idle children.**
1. Why did a child sit idle? It ended its turn to "wait" with no background job (REPORTED).
2. Why did nothing wake it? Only a background job or a message restarts a finished turn.
3. Root cause: the brief says "wait" and does not say how. Process gap, no class.

**Failure 4: duplicate PRs.**
1. Why did a child rebuild #7217, #7221, and #7224 work? It was assigned #7231, which listed items other children owned.
2. Why did it not see them? It did not search open PRs by sibling issue number first (REPORTED).
3. Root cause: the brief has no pre-build duplicate search. The memory `github/check-existing-pr-before-issue-worktree` already says to do this.

### Patterns and Shifts
- The 2026-10-12 sweep and this one failed the same way: an unbounded wave against a shared resource. Only the resource changed.
- Both retrospectives found the cap lived only in the orchestrator template. The 2026-10-12 owner finding on that point has no recorded decision.
