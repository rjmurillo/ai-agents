# Session Service Specification

## 3. Limits

A user may hold at most 3 active sessions at one time.
An idle session expires after 30 minutes.

## 5. Account policy

The concurrent session limit is 3 per account.
Sessions are listed on the account page.

## 6. Terms

An active user is anyone with a session.
