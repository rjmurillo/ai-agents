- Editing any `.github/workflows/*.yml` triggers a local `gh act` pre-push run that times out at 600 seconds on the author's machine; keep workflow edits out of large PRs.
- The pre-push `pre-pr-validation` job times out at 240 seconds under concurrent test load; push when no local suite is running.
- The retrospective policy refuses a push with no same-day retrospective under `.agents/retrospective/`.
- Merging a PR with `--delete-branch` closes every PR stacked on that branch and GitHub refuses to reopen it; retarget dependents first.
- CodeRabbit re-reviews on every push and can leave a stale `CHANGES_REQUESTED` review after its threads are resolved; a fresh review or a dismissal is needed before the ruleset lets the PR merge.
