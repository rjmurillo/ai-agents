# ADR-068 / ADR-071 / ADR-085 issue #5013 debate log

## Summary

- Scope: the 2026-08-14 exclusion of `push_pr_script_identity_guard` from the
  generated Copilot inventory, its ownership under ADR-085, and the derived
  ADR-068 and ADR-071 updates that follow from it.
- Rounds: 2.
- Outcome: consensus. Four agents accepted. Two agents
  disagreed-and-committed.

This log covers the issue #5013 containment and exclusion only. It is a
companion to `.agents/critique/ADR-068-071-085-metric-refresh-debate-log.md`,

## Round 1: agent findings

Six roles reviewed the initial issue #5013 draft, which recorded the Copilot
exclusion and the resulting shim and timeout counts but did not record why the
exclusion happened or who owns it. No verdict tally is recorded for this
round because the findings below identify missing content, not disputed
content; Phase 2 resolves each finding as a ruling rather than a vote.

| Agent | Finding |
|-------|---------|
| architect | ADR-085 recorded the exclusion outcome but not the incident that forced it. No denial count, no duration, and no root cause distinguishing a broad matcher registration from a timed child-process deny policy. A reader cannot tell whether containment was precautionary or reactive to a live failure. |
| critic | ADR-068 and ADR-071 both called the issue #5013 change a "facts refresh" while naming no policy owner. The phrase reads as a mechanical restatement, but the underlying decision, excluding a security guard from one harness, is a security ruling that neither ADR is positioned to make on its own. |
| independent-thinker | Decision 1's three-part eligibility test (portability, fidelity, policy safety) was written for hook-to-permissions migrations. Nothing in ADR-085 states whether or how that test applies to a same-harness, temporary exclusion, so the exclusion carried no recorded eligibility reasoning at all. |
| security | A generic `copilotExclude` boolean on a dispatch group is a reintroducible bypass mechanism with no attached governance. Nothing bounds its value type, requires issue or decision provenance, states the residual risk while it is set, or names what changes on the unaffected harness. The next unrelated exclusion could reuse the field with no review. |
| analyst | ADR-068's settings-registration count states seven registrations across five named events (SessionStart, UserPromptSubmit, PostToolUse, SessionEnd, PreCompact). `.claude/settings.json` registers six events, including `PostToolUseFailure`, which the prose omits while still claiming seven registrations. The count and the event list do not agree. |
| high-level-advisor | No debate log exists for this change. ADR-071's Status and Date sections do not mention the 2026-08-11 or 2026-08-14 amendments at all, though both amendments are fully written into the body. Reintroduction of the guard has no named owner, no approval requirement, and no measurable exit criteria; without gates, "temporary" has no way to end. |

## Phase 2: ruling

The owner ruled on scope and ownership before Round 1's findings were
individually re-litigated:

1. This is a cross-harness security decision owned by ADR-085, not a new ADR.
   ADR-068 and ADR-071 receive derived factual and runtime updates only, and
   both reference ADR-085 as the policy authority.
2. A generic `copilotExclude` field is allowed, but only with strict boolean
   validation, a named plugin surface, issue metadata, decision metadata, a
   stated residual risk, a stated unaffected-harness behavior, named
   reintroduction criteria, a named cleanup obligation, and required tests.
3. Issue #5013 and assignee rjmurillo own reintroduction. Reintroduction is
   optional and requires rjmurillo's approval; it is not a default outcome of
   passing the gates.
4. ADR-085 must record the containment incident (127 unrelated denials, more
   than 21 minutes, owner-required immediate containment), the root cause
   (broad `Bash` registration combined with a timed child-process deny, not a
   defect in the identity check), that Claude retains the guard unchanged,
   and that Copilot accepts the reopened `new_pr.py` lookalike gap as a named
   residual risk while the dispatcher itself is unchanged.
5. ADR-085 must apply Decision 1's eligibility test to this exclusion by
   analogy and state why containment passes temporarily and what security
   cost remains open.
6. All eight reintroduction gates from issue #5013 must be recorded in
   ADR-085: unrelated commands never launch the guard, the canonical
   `new_pr.py` invocation is allowed, a lookalike is denied, a dynamic
   launcher is denied, a Windows load test of 32 calls across 8 workers
   passes, latency stays under a 500ms p95 and a 1 second maximum, the
   measurement runs against a real Copilot CLI probe, and the owner
   classifies the guard as deleted or essential before it returns.
   Reintroduction stays optional and owner-approved.
7. Historical 110 and 115 second, three-shim statements from the 2026-08-11
   amendment stay in the record under their dated context. They are not
   errors to correct; they describe a prior true state.
